# Hall rules

Sections 1 to 11 come from the Declaration that every resident signs in the City University application form for residence in hall. Sections 12 and 13 explain how meals and complaints work in HallDesk. The hall authority can change these rules at any time.

## 1. Rent and payment

1. Pay the hall rent of the current month between the 1st and the {bill_due_day} of the month.
2. HallDesk creates the monthly bill with the seat rent of your room, an electricity charge of {electricity_charge} and the meals you took. It is due on the {bill_due_day}.
3. If you stay only part of a month, the rent and the electricity charge are counted by the days you stayed.
4. You can pay by bKash ({bkash_number}), Nagad ({nagad_number}), Rocket ({rocket_number}) or at the bank ({bank_account}). Then open Bills and payments, choose the bill and enter the amount and the transaction ID.
5. The hall office checks every payment. The bill shows as paid once the office has verified it.

## 2. Conduct and harmony

1. Live in harmony with the other residents.
2. Groupism of any type is not allowed.
3. Do not misbehave in any manner. This includes inappropriate language and physical tiffs or fights with other residents, hall employees or the local residents of the hall's neighbourhood.

## 3. Ragging

1. Ragging of any type is not allowed.
2. If you see or hear of ragging, bring it to the notice of the hall authority.

## 4. Alcohol and objectionable materials

1. Alcohol and other objectionable materials are strictly prohibited in the hall.

## 5. Anti-institutional and anti-social activity

1. Any anti-institutional or anti-social activity in the hall or on the campus is a punishable offence.

## 6. Cooking and electric appliances

1. Cooking is not allowed in the hall.
2. Do not use electric appliances that are prohibited in the hall. Ask the hall office which appliances are prohibited.

## 7. Hall property

1. Do not damage hall property in any way. This includes defacing furniture or changing the appearance of a room.
2. A resident who damages property will be penalised and punished.

## 8. Return time and staying out

1. Be inside the hall premises by the time the hall authority sets.
2. Do not stay out without proper permission from the hall authority beforehand.
3. This rulebook does not state the exact return time. Ask the hall office.

## 9. Health

1. A resident must be physically and medically fit to live in the hall.
2. Tell the university authority at once about any medical or psychological condition that could make you unfit to live in the hall.
3. The application form asks about infectious or chronic diseases, asthma, epilepsy, allergies, regular medicine and an emergency contact.

## 10. Your seat and leaving the hall

1. Vacate your seat at once if the hall authority asks you to, for any cause.
2. To leave the hall, fill in the Hall Clearance Form. Finish the clearance by the 10th of the month.
3. Clear all hall dues first. The Accounts Officer (Hall), the Hall Super, the Hall Admin and the Provost sign the clearance form.

## 11. Applying for a seat

1. Fill in the Application Form for Residence in Hall. The head or coordinator of your department recommends it.
2. Attach two recent passport size colour photographs, a copy of your National ID card and the bank receipt for the hall fee.
3. Ask the Hall Admin or the Hall Super whether a room is available. The Hall Admin or the Hall Super allots the room and the Provost confirms it.
4. Submit the form to the Hall Admin and note your form number.
5. A parent or guardian also signs that the student will follow the rules and has no objection to expulsion for breaking them.
6. In HallDesk you can also apply online from Apply for a seat and follow the status there.

## 12. Meals

1. Every resident gets lunch and dinner every day. Meals are ON unless you turn them off.
2. To skip a meal, turn it OFF in Meals, or ask the HallDesk assistant.
3. Changes for a date close at {meal_cutoff} on the day before. After that the date is locked, so the dining manager knows how much to cook.
4. Going home for a few days? Turn meals off for a range of dates, up to 60 days ahead. Locked dates are skipped.
5. You pay only for the meals that were ON. Lunch costs {lunch_rate} and dinner costs {dinner_rate}.
6. Meal charges are added to your monthly bill together with the rent.
7. Meals start from the day your seat starts and stop when you leave the hall.

## 13. Complaints

1. Report problems with electricity, water and plumbing, internet, furniture, cleanliness or security in Complaints, or ask the HallDesk assistant.
2. Choose a priority. A high priority complaint alerts the hall office at once.
3. Every complaint gets a number like C-0001. You can follow its progress, add comments and reopen it if the problem comes back.

## 14. Not covered by this rulebook

1. This rulebook does not cover visitors and guests, the exact return time, the list of prohibited appliances or the amount of hall fees.
2. For these, contact the hall office: {contact_phone}, {contact_email}.

## 15. Final word

1. Residents agree to follow all rules and regulations of the university and the terms and conditions for residence in hall, which may be changed from time to time.
2. The decision of the university authority is final in all respects.
